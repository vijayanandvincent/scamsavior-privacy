Privacy Policy — ScamSavior

Last updated: September 2026

ScamSavior ("the app") is developed by RegencyOps. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

What never leaves your device

The core function of ScamSavior — scanning SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Telegram X, Signal, Instagram, Messenger, Messenger Lite, Facebook, Facebook Lite, Samsung Messages, and incoming calls, for scam patterns — happens entirely on your phone. The text of your messages, call logs, sender names, phone numbers, and any words matched by our scam-detection engine are never transmitted anywhere. They are stored only in a local database on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What we never collect

  • Message or call content of any kind
  • Contacts, photos, files, or browsing history
  • Precise location (GPS)

Payments

Subscriptions are billed through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — Google processes all payments directly.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • SMS: to scan incoming text messages for scams, on-device only.
  • Notification access: to scan messages from RCS, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Telegram X, Signal, Instagram, Messenger, Messenger Lite, Facebook, Facebook Lite, and Samsung Messages — the exact list of apps we read is fixed in the app itself and nothing outside it is ever read — because Android does not expose their content any other way. On-device only.
  • Phone/Call state: to flag risky incoming calls by number pattern, on-device only.
  • Text-to-speech (voice alerts): reads a short warning aloud in your chosen language when a scam is detected. This uses your phone's built-in speech engine only — nothing is recorded and no audio ever leaves your device.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local scan history immediately.
  • Name/phone/email on the Account screen are optional — leave them blank if you prefer.
  • Contact us to request deletion of your aggregate account data: support@regencyops.com
